Questions

Good to know.

Do you travel to our hotel or location?

Yes. Tell us where you're staying or where you want to shoot. Nearby locations are included, and for longer trips we'll confirm any travel fee before you book.

Can you shoot for my YouTube vlog?

Yes. We film you on location, capture B-roll and drone shots, and can also edit the full vlog through our video editing service.

How soon do we get the photos and videos?

Quick Sessions are delivered in about 3 days, half-day shoots in about 5 days and full-day productions in about 7 days. Need it faster? Just ask.

What if the weather is bad?

We keep an eye on the forecast and will work with you to move the shoot to the next good slot.

Do we need to know how to pose?

Not at all. We guide you the whole time, and most of the best moments are natural ones.
