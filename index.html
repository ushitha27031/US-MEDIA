Questions

Good to know.

Still unsure? Message us on WhatsApp and we'll reply quickly.

How do I place an order?

Add services to your cart from the packages or the price list. Then open the cart and tap “Send order on WhatsApp”. Your full order and total are written out for you, so just press send. We'll confirm the details and get started.

Can I order script, voice, video and thumbnail together?

Yes. Pick one of the ready-made packages, or add each service to your cart one by one. Everything is sent as one order.

How do I send my footage?

Once we've confirmed your order on WhatsApp, share your files with a Google Drive, Dropbox or WeTransfer link. If we're writing the script and voice over, just send your topic or idea.

Are revisions included?

Yes. Every order comes with revisions. We keep working until you're happy with it.

What's the difference between AI and Human voice or script?

AI options are fast and budget-friendly, which suits high-volume channels. Human options are written or voiced by a real person, with more personality, better storytelling and a natural delivery.

Who handles Channel Management & Boosting?

Our trusted growth partner. If it's in your cart, you'll see a separate button to send that part of your order straight to them on WhatsApp.
